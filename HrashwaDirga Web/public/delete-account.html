Account Management

Delete your account

If you would like to permanently remove your Hrashwa Dirga account and all associated data, please complete the form below. This action is irreversible.

Before you continue

Deleting your account will permanently remove the following data. This cannot be undone.

  • Your username, avatar, and all profile information
  • Your complete game progress, level history, and score
  • Your coin balance and any earned rewards
  • Your position on the global and country leaderboards
  • Your daily streak record and all associated bonuses
How to request account deletion
  • 1
    Fill in your registered email address — the one linked to your Google account used to sign in to Hrashwa Dirga.
  • 2
    Enter the username associated with your account (up to 10 characters, as shown on the leaderboard).
  • 3
    Provide a reason for your deletion request in at least 100 characters to help us improve the experience for other players.
  • 4
    Submit the form. Our team will verify your identity and process the request. We will respond within 48 hours.

Request already submitted

You have already submitted an account deletion request. Our team will process it and respond to your registered email within 48 hours. Please do not submit duplicate requests.
