Effective: January 1, 2024

01

Introduction

Hrashwa Dirga ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and associated services.

By using Hrashwa Dirga, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not access the application.

02

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us and information generated through your use of the application.

  • Google account information (name, email address, profile picture) obtained through Google Sign-In
  • Username you choose within the application (up to 10 characters)
  • Game progress data including levels completed, score, and streak count
  • Country/region information for leaderboard filtering purposes
  • Device information and operating system version for compatibility purposes
03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve Hrashwa Dirga. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Create and manage your player account and authenticate your identity via Google Sign-In
  • Display your username and auto-generated avatar on the global leaderboard
  • Calculate and update your rank, score, and progress across game sessions
  • Sync your game data across devices associated with your Google account
  • Provide customer support and respond to your inquiries
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activity or abuse of the platform
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve gameplay features and performance
04

Leaderboard and Public Information

Hrashwa Dirga is a competitive knowledge game. Part of its core functionality involves displaying player information publicly.

Your username, auto-generated avatar, country/region, score, number of levels completed, and global rank are visible to all users of the application on the leaderboard screen.

Your email address is never displayed publicly and is only used internally for account management, communication, and support purposes. If you wish to keep all your information private, you should not use the leaderboard features of the application.

05

Google Sign-In and Third-Party Services

We use Google Sign-In to authenticate users. When you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information from your Google account as permitted by Google's own privacy policies and your account settings. We do not receive or store your Google account password.

We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy to understand how Google handles your information. Our use of Google Sign-In is subject to Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

06

Data Retention

We retain your account information and game data for as long as your account remains active. If you request deletion of your account, we will process your request and remove your personally identifiable information from our active systems within 30 days of verification.

Some anonymized, aggregated data may be retained for analytical purposes even after account deletion. Leaderboard history may retain anonymized score records without any association to your identity.

07

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your Google account and to enable two-factor authentication on your Google account to further protect your data.

08

Children's Privacy

Hrashwa Dirga is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary action.

09

Your Rights and Account Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal information at any time. You may also request a copy of the data we hold about you.

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, please use our dedicated account deletion page. We will verify your identity and process your request promptly.

  • Access or correct your personal information through the app profile settings
  • Request complete account deletion via our account deletion page
  • Withdraw consent for data processing, subject to certain legal requirements
  • Lodge a complaint with your relevant data protection authority
10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the effective date at the top. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Your continued use of Hrashwa Dirga after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
